Open positions

Board Member: Finance & Legal Manager

  • Keeps Drupal Switzerland association books up to date
  • Manages contracts with event venues, such as for Drupal Mountain Camp

Volunteer: Lead Public Relations

  • Promote Drupal in the Swiss market
  • Develop the brand and image of the community

Volunteer: Social Media Manager / Marketeer for Splash Awards

  • Promoting the Splash Awards on the Social Media Channels as well as in the community with regular Updates. Supporting in the creation of Marketing Materials
  • Do Interviews with previous Award Winners (clients) to generate Marketing Materials
  • Helping with the After Awards Communication (Writing Summaries, deploying to Newspapers, promote Sponsors, promote the event and its participants

Volunteer: Video Producer / Animation Manager

  • Creating Introduction Videos for our Projects
  • Creating Supporting Videos during the Splash Awards pre Face

Thank you for sending us a quick note to info@drupal.ch if you would like to fill one of the open positions.

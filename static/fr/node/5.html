Impressum

The Drupal Switzerland community and website is brought to you by the Drupal Switzerland.

The association aims to promote the acquaintance of the open-source Content Management System Drupal by organizing events. The association takes into account its services and is - on request - a general contractor for Drupal relevant events in Switzerland.
